Subject: Approval of Taxi Pool Feature for Phase 1 Release

Hi Everyone,

Following the stakeholder review meeting held on 14 May 2026 and subsequent technical discussions, I am formally approving the Taxi Pool feature for inclusion in the Phase 1 release of PullUp.

The decision is based on the feedback collected from students, product review sessions, and technical feasibility assessments completed by the development team.

Approved Scope:

1. Taxi Pool Creation

Students should be able to create and join transportation pools based on destination and departure time.

2. Destination Clustering

Matching should be based on transportation zones rather than exact destination matches.

3. Pool Chat

Each pool should include a temporary communication channel for coordination.

4. Pool Status Tracking

Pools should support statuses including:

* Searching
* Forming
* Confirmed
* Ride Active
* Completed

5. Fare Splitting Calculator

Users should be able to enter the total fare and view the calculated share per participant.

The following features are explicitly excluded from Phase 1:

* Automated payments
* Direct Uber integration
* Direct Ola integration
* Driver tracking
* Multi-campus support
* AI-powered recommendations

These items may be considered in future releases following validation of the core pooling experience.

The primary objective of Phase 1 remains unchanged:

Reduce transportation costs for students by helping them share rides efficiently.

Please proceed with implementation planning based on the approved scope.

Thank you to everyone involved in the review process.

Regards,
