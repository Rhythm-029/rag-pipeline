Subject: Change Request – Destination Clustering Instead of Exact Matching

Hi Team,

After reviewing the proposed Taxi Pool feature and discussing matching behaviour with students, I would like to submit an additional change request regarding ride matching.

The current matching concept assumes that students will be matched based on exact destination entries.

I am concerned that this approach may result in very low match rates.

For example:

Student A may enter:
"Hiranandani Estate"

Student B may enter:
"Viviana Mall"

Student C may enter:
"Teen Hath Naka"

Technically these are different locations.

However, all three students are effectively travelling to Thane.

Under an exact matching model, these users would never be connected despite having highly compatible transportation needs.

To improve matching efficiency, I would like the team to evaluate a destination clustering approach.

The proposed approach should group nearby destinations into larger transportation zones.

Examples:

Thane Cluster

* Hiranandani Estate
* Viviana Mall
* Teen Hath Naka
* Majiwada

Navi Mumbai Cluster

* Vashi
* Nerul
* Belapur
* Kharghar

Western Suburbs Cluster

* Andheri
* Bandra
* Kandivali
* Borivali

The objective is to maximize successful pool formation rather than pursue perfect destination accuracy.

Please assess:

* Technical feasibility
* Impact on user experience
* Impact on Firebase architecture
* Impact on Google Maps usage
* Potential risks

I believe this change will significantly improve the success rate of Taxi Pool formation and increase overall user satisfaction.

Regards,

Krish

Founder, PullUp
